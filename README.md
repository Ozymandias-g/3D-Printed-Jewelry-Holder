# 3D Printed Meshgrid Jewelry Holder ✨💍

My sister needed a better way to organize her necklaces and rings, so I grabbed a ruler, took some measurements, and modeled this custom meshgrid holder for her. 

## The Design
* **Mesh Grid:** Perfect for hanging necklaces, bracelets, and earrings without them getting tangled. 
* **Ring Holder:** A dedicated horizontal shelf section at the bottom specifically designed to hold rings securely. 

## The "Oops" Moment (Mounting)
The original design included hooks meant to hang seamlessly over the top of her cabinet door. Unfortunately, the 3D-printed hooks couldn't handle the stress and cracked. As a quick and sturdy workaround, I ended up drilling through the broken hook remnants and attaching the holder directly to the cabinet door using screws. 

Sometimes engineering is just adapting on the fly!
